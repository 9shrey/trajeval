# Kestrel Cycles: Customer Policy Handbook (v4, July 2025)

## Returns and refunds
- Accessories (helmets, chargers) can be returned unused within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
- E-bikes can be returned within 14 days of delivery if the odometer shows less than 50 km.
- Service plans are refundable within 7 days of purchase if no service has been used.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of approval.

## Warranty
- Frame: 5 years.
- Motor: 2 years.
- Battery: 2 years or 800 charge cycles, whichever comes first.
- Wear parts (tyres, brake pads, chain) are not covered.

## Shipping
- Metro cities (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune): 3-5 business days.
- All other locations: 7-10 business days.
- Shipping is free on orders above Rs 5,000; otherwise a flat Rs 249 fee applies.

## Support
- Support hours: Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 19:00 IST.
- First-response targets: high priority 4 hours, medium priority 1 business day, low priority 3 business days.
- Tickets open for more than 10 days must be escalated to the Head of Support.
