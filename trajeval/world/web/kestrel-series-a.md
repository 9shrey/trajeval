title: Kestrel Cycles raises $12 million Series A

4 March 2025. Bengaluru e-bike maker Kestrel Cycles has raised $12 million in a Series A
round led by Banyan Peak Ventures, with participation from Orbit Capital. Founded in 2021 by
Farah Qureshi and Tenzin Dorje, Kestrel sells the Trailblazer and CityHopper lines online and
plans to use the money to open experience centres in 20 cities and build a battery-pack
assembly line in Pune. The company says revenue tripled in 2024.
