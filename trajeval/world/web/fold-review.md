title: CityHopper Fold review: light enough for the metro

Rating: 7.4/10. The CityHopper Fold folds in about 15 seconds and weighs 17.5 kg. We measured
45 km of range against a claimed 50 km. The small 20-inch wheels feel twitchy at speed and the
display is hard to read in sunlight, but for metro-plus-last-mile commuters it is the most
practical bike in Kestrel's range.
