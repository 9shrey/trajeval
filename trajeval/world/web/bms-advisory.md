title: Battery firmware update v2.3.2 available

Battery management firmware v2.3.2 is now rolling out over the air to all Battery Pack 48V
owners. It fixes a range-estimate error introduced in v2.3.1. No action is needed: bikes
update automatically when connected to the app over Wi-Fi.
