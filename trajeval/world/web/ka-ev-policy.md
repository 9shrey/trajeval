title: Karnataka EV Promotion Scheme 2025: e-bicycle purchase subsidy

Under the Karnataka EV Promotion Scheme 2025, residents of Karnataka buying a new electric
bicycle receive a purchase subsidy of 15% of the ex-showroom price, capped at Rs 10,000 per
bicycle. Only bicycles with an ex-showroom price below Rs 1,00,000 qualify. The scheme runs
from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026. The subsidy is credited by the dealer at the time of sale.
