title: About Voltcell Energy

Voltcell Energy is a lithium-ion cell manufacturer founded in 2016 in Pune by Nandini Rao,
who is still its chief executive. The company makes 21700-format cells for electric two-wheelers
and e-bikes at a 2 GWh plant in Chakan. Voltcell supplies about 40 Indian EV makers and
became profitable in 2023. In 2025 it announced a second plant, due in 2027.
