title: Sparrow Motors launches Swift E2 e-bike at Rs 54,999

September 2025. Hyderabad-based Sparrow Motors has launched the Swift E2 electric bicycle at
Rs 54,999, undercutting most commuter e-bikes. Sparrow claims 60 km of range and a 3-hour
charge time. Analysts see it as a direct rival to Kestrel's CityHopper S.
