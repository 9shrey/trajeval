title: Kestrel Trailblazer X1 review: a capable commuter with optimistic range

Rating: 8.2/10. The Trailblazer X1 is the best-built e-bike we have tested under Rs 1 lakh.
Kestrel claims 80 km of range; in our mixed city test we got 68 km on the middle assist level.
A full charge takes 4.5 hours with the bundled charger, or 2.5 hours with the Fast Charger.
The bike weighs 24 kg, which makes carrying it up stairs a chore. Pros: solid frame, good
app, hydraulic brakes. Cons: heavy, range below the claim, no front suspension lockout.
