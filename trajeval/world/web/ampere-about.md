title: Ampere Works: company profile

Ampere Works was founded in 2012 in Chennai by Harish Venkat (CEO) and started out making
motor controllers for electric rickshaws. It began producing 21700 lithium-ion cells in 2024
and is ramping a 1.2 GWh line. Its cells are rated at 5.0 Ah, among the highest-capacity
21700 cells made in India.
