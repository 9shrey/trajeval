title: Bengaluru Cycle Day 2025

Bengaluru Cycle Day 2025 takes place on Sunday 16 November at Cubbon Park, from 6:30 to
11:00. Registration is free. This year's sponsors are Kestrel Cycles and Namma Metro, and
Kestrel will run free e-bike test rides near the bandstand.
